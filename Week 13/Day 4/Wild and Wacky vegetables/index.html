Vegetables come in a remarkable range of colors, textures, and flavors. Roast root vegetables to bring out their sweetness, add leafy greens to soups, or enjoy crunchy vegetables fresh with a favorite dip.

Beets, carrots, squash, mushrooms, and celery root each bring something special to a meal. Try an unfamiliar vegetable the next time you visit the market and discover a new favorite.
